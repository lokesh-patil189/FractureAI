Problem statement

Small fractures can be hard to spot.

Bone fractures in X-ray images can be difficult to identify when the fracture is small, unclear, or in a complex area. Manual review requires expertise and can take time. This project explores an AI-based system that detects suspected fractures and estimates their location to support faster preliminary analysis by medical professionals.

✦

Our mission
Explore responsible AI assistance for preliminary fracture screening — not automated diagnosis or treatment advice.

Questions & data

Clear answers.
Respectful handling.

BoneAI is a student research prototype. Review these limits before uploading anything, especially medical images.

Does BoneAI guarantee an accurate diagnosis?

No. The app cannot guarantee 100% accuracy and is not a diagnostic service. It requires a validated model for screening output, and a qualified medical professional must review any image and result.

Does this package include a trained fracture model?

No. It includes the web workflow and a model adapter, but no trained weights or dataset. Without weights, the app will save and preprocess an upload and clearly report that inference is unavailable.

Where are uploaded files stored?

In this prototype, images and the SQLite database are stored on the app server under the configured instance directory. The files are access-controlled by account, but production-grade encryption, backup and retention controls still need to be configured before handling sensitive data.

Can I remove an uploaded image?

Yes. Sign in, open My history, and choose Delete for the record. The app removes the associated image file from its local upload directory.

◈PRIVACY FIRST

Use test data for demos.

Do not upload identifiable patient information to a public demo. Prefer synthetic, public, or properly de-identified images, and follow your institution’s rules.

  • Account-scoped image routes
  • Delete control in history
  • No external AI API calls by default
